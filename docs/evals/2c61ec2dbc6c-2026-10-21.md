## How to read this report

- **Rate rule** (NFR-QUAL-8): a rate metric passes when its mean across seeds clears the floor by
  more than one sample standard deviation (≥ floors: mean - SD above the floor; ≤ floors: mean +
  SD below it). One seed, or no observations, is *insufficient evidence*.
- **Observed-zero rule** (NFR-QUAL-8): a zero-tolerance metric passes only with no violation on any
  seed, over at least 300 labeled cases. The bound column gives the one-sided 95% upper bound on
  the true rate (≈ 3/n): a clean run is evidence of a low rate, not proof of a zero one.
- **Relative rule** (NFR-QUAL-14): an engine-intervention type passes when its first-draft
  preference rate's mean across seeds plus one SD is at most the floor, judged only on at least
  300 non-tied pairs per seed; fewer is *insufficient evidence*, never a pass. It is an aggregate
  target: individual messages may still read worse than their drafts.
- A judge-based row is only as good as the judge: before such a run the quality judge is
  calibrated against human-labeled pairs, and a judge whose agreement is below 75% makes every
  judge-based row *report only*.
- A model error during a case counts as a failed case; it is never excluded. Timings are
  informational: the model may share its machine with other work.
- A floor applies to the configuration this fingerprint names (NFR-QUAL-11); any other
  configuration is unmeasured until it has its own report.
- E-10 scores detection only: a labeled claim is detected when a detected claim on its beat covers at least half of the shorter span with a compatible category (any extraction claim is compatible). A composed message is detected with extraction forced on, as the pipeline does, so it counts alike under both modes. The `identity-declared` family puts an identity claim in a beat that declares something else: no detector flags it, so `gated` extraction never reads it (a measured gap).
- E-01's label is the final kind a correct check ends with; `uncertain` marks a claim neither canon nor world knowledge decides (the safe disposition). About 30 % of the cases are the fuzzy residual, the only ones that reach the model (an approximate-bounds residual does not: it ends `uncertain` with no call); the `e01.residual.*` rows show them alone.
- E-04 has no floor (#132 lists importance as `unmeasured`): `e04.spearman` is the per-seed rank correlation between the score and the pivotal (1) / trivial (0) label, with tied ranks averaged; its n is the cases scored.
- Q-02 resolves each query through the retrieval pipeline's resolve stage, with no model; docs/04 sets no floor, so its rows are report-only.
- Q-01's ranking is what a render's context receives in order: the located occasion's episodes, the assessments, then the ranked memories. Recall@10 is micro-averaged over every query's relevant beats. Without an embedding model (`--embedding-model`) the vector arm does not run and recall rests on the lexical and alias arms.
- E-05 counts a predicted salient span as a hit when it is on the labeled span's beat and shares at least half of the shorter span; recall and precision are micro-averaged over spans per seed, and `e05.f_beta3` weights a missed salient span three times a false one.
- E-03's judge is the `entailment` purpose. On a single-model run it is the model under test judging its own reflections. With `--judge-model` (#475) it is that second model, and the suite's Judge line names it. `e03.judge_agreement` shows how well the judge judges the labeled entailed and non-entailed reflections. A window that produced no reflection is not counted in `nfr_qual_3.faithfulness`, but is in `e03.produced`.
- Q-03 compares recall of an abstract question with and without the reflection tier; no floor applies (docs/04). Without an embedding model retrieval is lexical, so the arm without reflections finds only deeds that share a word with the question.
- E-07 injects unparseable render answers into 10 % of its cases (the first attempt, or every attempt) by wrapping the model adapter for those cases only; `e07.no_silent_acceptance` must be 100 %: such a turn fails with `invalid_structure` and commits nothing.
- `nfr_qual_5.valid_after_retry` counts only the render's structured-call validity (`invalid_output` attempts retried to a valid answer); a safe fallback or deterministic repair released after a valid render is not a miss (the owner's decision on #465).
- E-06 (M4) counts a case as conforming only when the structure is valid, the message is within 125 % of its length's token budget (four characters a token), and, for the temperature-0 `repeat` cases, a second story gives the same render call output (the rendered beats, a repaired beat by its original text; not the composed message: the owner's decision on #464).
- E-12 (M4) pairs each turn's first draft (its raw renders, assembled deterministically) with its final message wherever a repair rung, the composition or its polish changed it; names, places, dates and quantities are masked alike in both, and the judge compares them in both orders on voice and readability only (a disagreement is a tie).
