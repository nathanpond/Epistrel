# `judge_calibration`: provenance

- **Measures:** the quality judge's agreement with human preference labels before any judge-based result is certified (E-12, NFR-QUAL-14; `epistrel evals calibrate-judge`). The floor is 75 % agreement on at least 200 pairs, reported with its Wilson 95 % interval.
- **Labeled by:** the builder (`labeled_by: builder` on every row; the owner's round-one decision on #158: the builder writes the preference labels, the owner approves them), on 2026-10-20, for #158.
- **How:** 220 pairs of passages written for the same moment, compared on voice consistency and readability (`criteria: voice_readability`), facts set aside. 180 pairs put a deliberate defect on one side, 36 per family: a generic safe-fallback line (`fallback-line`), a passage cut off mid-clause (`broken`), self-repetition (`repetition`), a jarring register (`register`) and stock filler (`stock`). The intact passage is the preferred one. A seeded draw puts it on side `a` or `b` (96 `a`, 84 `b`). 40 pairs are one-word paraphrases of the same passage, labeled `tie` (`equivalent`). Each row names the characters the judge sees masked (`names`).
- **What it does not measure:** the labels follow from how each pair is built. The set checks that the judge sees visible defects and does not invent preferences between equivalent passages. It does not measure agreement on subtle differences between two good passages, so a calibrated judge is evidence of a usable judge, not of a perfect one.
- **Generated by:** `src/epistrel/evals/authoring/judge_calibration.py` (deterministic, template-driven; no model). `uv run python -m epistrel.evals author` regenerates the set, and `--check-sets` fails when the committed file differs.
- **Review:** the owner reviews the digest (`docs/evals/sets-digest.md`) and records approval by replacing the line below with `approved by owner YYYY-MM-DD` in a commit of their own (`approved_by: owner <date>`). Until then `--check-sets` passes on a branch and fails where approval is required (`EPISTREL_REQUIRE_APPROVED_SETS=1`).
