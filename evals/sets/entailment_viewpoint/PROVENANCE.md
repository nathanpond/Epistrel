# `entailment_viewpoint`: provenance

- **Measures:** E-01 M5 (`e01_viewpoint`): viewpoint-aware entailment, NFR-QUAL-2's false-contradiction and false-support rates over a speaker's own claims (`nfr_qual_2.viewpoint.*`; the floors gate from M9, ADR-164).
- **Authored by:** the builder (the owner's round-one decision on #157, kept for #179: the builder labels, the owner approves), on 2026-10-23, for #179 (completing #166's acceptance criterion 5).
- **How:** 300 (claim, canon, viewpoint → verdict) cases, 100 per family: `lie` (the speaker believes the truth and says otherwise, deceptively; canon contradicts the claim, settles nothing, or supports it as an attempted lie that is true; and a deception inferred from a `secret` contrary belief), `false_belief` (the speaker sincerely says what it wrongly believes) and `consistent` (the speaker says what it rightly believes, including a declared lie the speaker actually believes, relabelled sincere). Each case gives canon, the speaker's belief partition and the declared intent, in E-01's vocabulary of names, real geography and years; each family cycles five or six hand-written templates whose names and years vary from a seeded chooser. The label is the classification #166 owes the claim (`lie`, `sincere_error`, `sincere`), its belief relation and its objective verdict; none is repaired, so a repair is a false contradiction. Before committing, every case was checked against the verifier (#142, #166): all 300 end as labeled, and the canon side of every case is deterministic. No narrator cases.
- **Generated by:** `src/epistrel/evals/authoring/entailment_viewpoint.py` (deterministic, template-driven; no model). `uv run python -m epistrel.evals author` regenerates the set; `--check-sets` fails when the committed file differs, so the set changes only through its reviewed module. Every case carries its `origin`: `authored` or `paraphrase`.
- **Review:** the owner reviews the digest (`docs/evals/sets-digest.md`, stratified by family and template) and records approval by replacing the line below with `approved by owner YYYY-MM-DD` in a commit of their own. Until then `--check-sets` passes on a branch and fails where approval is required (`EPISTREL_REQUIRE_APPROVED_SETS=1`).
