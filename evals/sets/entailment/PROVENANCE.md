# `entailment`: provenance

- **Measures:** E-01 (`e01`): entailment accuracy, NFR-QUAL-2.
- **Authored by:** the builder (the owner's round-one decision on #157: the builder labels, the owner approves), on 2026-10-19, for #157.
- **How:** 420 claims against objective canon written in a small vocabulary of names and years: 60 per deterministic rule family (coverage, polarity, functional, structural, novel) and 120 in the fuzzy residual (world knowledge, approximate bounds, weak semantics). Each family cycles through hand-written templates; a template's first instance is `authored` (the first coverage, polarity, functional, novel and world-knowledge templates' first instance is the whitepaper's Bobby-in-Ohio education fixture), the others vary names, places and years from a seeded chooser (`paraphrase`). The label is the final kind a correct check ends with: `supported`, `contradicted`, `novel`, or `uncertain` where neither canon nor world knowledge decides the claim. Places and their containment are real geography, so the world-knowledge cases have a true answer. Before committing, every non-residual label was checked against the deterministic rules (#140) and every residual case confirmed to escalate.
- **Generated by:** `src/epistrel/evals/authoring/entailment.py` (deterministic, template-driven; no model). `uv run python -m epistrel.evals author` regenerates the set; `--check-sets` fails when the committed file differs, so the set changes only through its reviewed module. Every case carries its `origin`: `whitepaper`, `authored` or `paraphrase`.
- **Review:** the owner reviews the digest (`docs/evals/sets-digest.md`) and records approval by replacing the line below with `approved by owner YYYY-MM-DD` in a commit of their own. Until then `--check-sets` passes on a branch and fails where approval is required (`EPISTREL_REQUIRE_APPROVED_SETS=1`).
