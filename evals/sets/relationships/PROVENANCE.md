# `relationships`: provenance

- **Measures:** Q-06 (`q06`): relationship recall, FR-RET-6. Informational: every row is report-only, there is no floor, and the suite never gates. It exists to compare `memory.episodic.profile_scope` runs (`message`, the default, against `full`, through `run --dial memory.episodic.profile_scope=full`; #905, ADR-193).
- **Authored by:** the builder (the owner's go on #913, 2026-11-14: the builder labels, the owner approves), on 2026-11-14, for #913.
- **How:** one world, Gullhaven's inn, the Gull (`src/epistrel/evals/relationship_world.py`). The player Wren asks the innkeeper Maren about the town's people, and Maren renders. Eight townspeople are tied by 8 objective relationships (`married_to`, `parent_of`, `sibling_of`, `friend_of`, `enemy_of`), and Maren has 3 of her own. No character definition mentions a relationship. 46 questions in four kinds:
  - `undescribed` (11, asked at both depths: 22 cases): a question about one end of a relationship that points at the other end by a verb or a plural ("Maren, who is Bobby married to?", "who are Bobby's parents?"). Retrieval's resolve stage does not resolve that to a person, so a `message`-scoped profile leaves the relationship out.
  - `described` (13, deep): the same relationships with a relation noun of `core/resolution/lexicon.json` ("who is Bobby's spouse?"). The resolve stage resolves the noun to the person, and both settings keep the relationship. A description with two answers (Jory's two children) is left out, because the resolver leaves an ambiguous description unresolved.
  - `named` (8, deep): both ends and a distractor named ("Maren, is Bobby married to Carol or to Tamsin?"), with the right answer first in odd cases.
  - `speaker` (3, deep): the answer is Maren herself ("who is Jory's friend?"). She is the render's speaker and stays in scope.

  **Depth.** In a `deep` case the asked person first knows 24 townsfolk, which is more current canon than retrieval reads about one entity (20). The canon arm then carries no relationship, so the remaining routes are the standing profile, which the setting governs, and the fact's own semantic memory. A `shallow` case may also be carried by the canon arm.

  Each case lists its expected answer forms (the answer's name; for `speaker`, `me` and `myself`) and, for `named`, the distractor. Each carries the stub's answer, which states the expected answer.
- **Generated by:** `src/epistrel/evals/authoring/relationships.py` (deterministic, template-driven; no model). `uv run python -m epistrel.evals author` regenerates the set; `--check-sets` fails when the committed file differs. The generator refuses a question that names its own answer (a named case aside), a question the world gives no answer, and a speaker question whose answer is anyone but Maren.
- **Scoring:** deterministic, in the suite (`suites/q06_relationships.py`), in this order:
  1. an expected name → correct (for a named case that also names its distractor → unclear);
  2. a don't-know phrase → don't-know;
  3. a speaker's self-reference → correct;
  4. another cast name → wrong;
  5. otherwise unclear.

  No model judges an answer.
- **Not covered:** relationships among more than a handful of people, relationships that changed over time, beliefs about relationships (a character's private view), and the narrator's view.
- **Review:** the owner reviews the digest (`docs/evals/sets-digest.md`, the `relationships/cases.jsonl` section) and records approval by replacing the line below with `approved by owner YYYY-MM-DD` in a commit of their own. Until then `--check-sets` passes on a branch and fails where approval is required (`EPISTREL_REQUIRE_APPROVED_SETS=1`). A content change after approval resets the line to pending. The set is approved before a live run's report counts.
