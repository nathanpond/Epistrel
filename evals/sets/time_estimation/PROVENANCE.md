Seven activity families: meals 29, movies 29, drives 29, flights 29, sleep 28, waits 28, work shifts 28. Each family cycles hand-written templates of three kinds, named at the start of each case's `notes`:
  - `explicit` (67): the sentence states the amount ("Twenty minutes later, …", "a ten-hour shift"); the range is the amount ± 10 %, at least ± 2 minutes.
  - `implicit` (81): no amount; the activity sets it ("They finish dinner and push back their chairs.", "The flight from London to New York lands."); the range is what such an activity ordinarily takes, judged per template (a sit-down dinner 40–150, a feature film 80–160, London to New York 420–540, a night's sleep 360–600).
  - `inferred` (52): the established times give a start, a length or an end, and the sentence runs to it (a two-hour film that started at 19:10, the clock at 19:40, "They watch until the credits roll.": 90); the range is the remaining time ± 5 % for an exact end (an alarm, a timetable, a closing time; at least ± 3 minutes) or ± 10 % for a typical length (a dinner's, a drive's, a match's; at least ± 10 minutes).
  Names, places, titles, amounts and clock times are drawn from a seeded chooser; the activity words are those of #199's `ACTIVITY_LEXICON` (eat, cook, watch, drive, travel, sleep, wait, work), so a live turn would establish the same activities. Each case carries the stub's scripted answer (`stub_answer`: the range's midpoint at confidence 0.9), which the digest leaves out.
